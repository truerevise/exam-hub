(A) Gond
(B) Koya
(C) Lambada
(D) Chenchu

(A-Telugu) గోండ్
(B-Telugu) కోయ
(C-Telugu) లంబాడా
(D-Telugu) చెంచు
Telugu Explanation: లంబాడీలు (సుగాలీలు, బంజారాలు అని కూడా పిలుస్తారు) ఈ ప్రొఫైల్‌లో తెలంగాణలో అతిపెద్ద ST సమూహంగా చూపబడ్డారు. 2020 శిక్షణ పత్రం ప్రకారం వారి జనాభా 20,44,039 (ST జనాభాలో 64.32%). ఇవి 2011 జనగణన ఆధారిత చారిత్రక గణాంకాలు.
English Question: In the Telangana Scheduled Tribe population profile based on Census 2011, which community is the largest?
Answer: C
Explanation: Lambadis (also listed as Sugalis and Banjaras) are the largest ST community in the Telangana profile cited in the 2020 MCRHRDI/Tribal Welfare training material. That table reports 2,044,039 persons, or 64.32% of the ST population. The figures are historical Census 2011-based statistics, not a current population estimate.

English Question: Which of the following best describes why a community's Scheduled Tribe status is state-specific?
Answer: A
Explanation: Article 342 provides for ST specification in relation to a State or Union Territory. Inclusion or exclusion from the constitutional ST list is a matter for Parliament by law, following the constitutional process. A community's recognition in one state does not automatically establish ST status in every other state.
