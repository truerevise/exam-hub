Q1. In the Telangana Scheduled Tribe population profile based on Census 2011, which community is the largest?
(A) Gond
(B) Koya
(C) Lambada
(D) Chenchu

Explanation: Lambadis (also listed as Sugalis and Banjaras) are the largest ST community in the Telangana profile cited in the 2020 MCRHRDI/Tribal Welfare training material. Q19. Which of the following constitutional provisions empowers the President to specify Scheduled Tribes in relation to a State or Union Territory?
(A) Article 330
(B) Article 338A
(C) Article 342
(D) Article 244
Answer: C
Explanation: Article 342 provides the constitutional procedure for specification of Scheduled Tribes. The President specifies the communities by public notification for a State or Union Territory, and Parliament may include or exclude communities by law. Article 338A concerns the National Commission for Scheduled Tribes, while Article 244 concerns administration under the Fifth and Sixth Schedules.

Q24. PESA stands for:
